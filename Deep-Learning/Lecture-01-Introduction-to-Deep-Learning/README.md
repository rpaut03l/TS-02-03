# Lecture 01 — Introduction to Deep Learning

`[← Deep Learning Hub](../README.md) · [🔝 Top](#lecture-01--introduction-to-deep-learning)`

**Instructor:** Dr. Anushka Joshi, IIT Jodhpur | **Date:** April 2026 | **Source slides:** L1 "Introduction to Deep Learning and CNN" (pages 1–30 of the merged deck)

This lecture answers one question: *why does Deep Learning — and specifically CNNs — even exist?* It covers the history and applications of Deep Learning, the three levels of vision tasks, single- vs multi-channel data types, why plain fully-connected networks fail on images, and the two big ideas (invariance and locality) that motivate convolution.
